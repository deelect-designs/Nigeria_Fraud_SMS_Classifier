Common SMS Scams in Nigeria

Our AI is designed to recognize patterns commonly used by fraudsters targeting Nigerians.

Fake Bank Alerts

Messages pretending to come from GTBank, Access Bank, UBA, First Bank, Zenith Bank and others.

BVN & NIN Scams

Fake verification requests asking for personal information or payment.

Lottery & Prize Scams

Messages claiming you've won money and requesting processing fees.

POS & Transfer Fraud

Fake transfer notifications asking victims to return money.

🇳🇬 SMS Safety Tips for Nigerians

Protect yourself against common SMS fraud techniques.

Avoid Unknown Links

Never click links from unexpected SMS messages, even if they appear to come from your bank.

Verify With Your Bank

Always contact your bank using its official phone number before responding.

Protect Your Information

Never share your BVN, OTP, ATM PIN or password by SMS.

Report Fraud

Report suspicious messages to your bank or the relevant authorities.
